Margin notes5 suggestions
DICTION

“nature can offer comfort and a different perspective on life”

→

Try naming the actual shift: “nature becomes a source of interpretive clarity.”

EVIDENCE

“This is important because it contrasts...”

→

Replace the placeholder with the effect: “The hush makes the speaker's earlier confusion audible by contrast.”

STRUCTURE

“The speaker uses imagery to show this change.”

→

Let this claim do more work: “The poem's light imagery charts a movement from observation to recognition.”

SOPHISTICATION

“has found some kind of clarity”

→

Keep the uncertainty, but complicate it: “finds clarity without resolving the need that made it necessary.”

GRAMMAR

“nature is just a background, but later it becomes...”

→

Use a stronger pivot and avoid the vague “something”: “nature shifts from backdrop to interlocutor.”
